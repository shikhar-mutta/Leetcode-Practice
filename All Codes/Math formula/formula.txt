[1] nCr (Combinations)
    nCr = nC(r-1) * (n - r + 1) / r

----------------------------------------

[2] Pascal's Triangle - Nth Row
    The Nth row contains:
    C(N-1, 0),  C(N-1, 1),  C(N-1, 2),  ...  C(N-1, N-1)

----------------------------------------

[3] Area of Triangle (Shoelace Formula)
    Given 3 points: A(x1,y1), B(x2,y2), C(x3,y3)

    Area = |x1*(y2 - y3) + x2*(y3 - y1) + x3*(y1 - y2)| / 2
