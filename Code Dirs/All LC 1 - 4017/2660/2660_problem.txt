 2660. Determine the Winner of a Bowling Game
 Easy  |  Tags: Array, Simulation
 https://leetcode.com/problems/determine-the-winner-of-a-bowling-game/description/
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PROBLEM
  You are given two 0-indexed integer arrays player1 and player2,
  representing the number of pins that player 1 and player 2 hit in a
  bowling game, respectively.

  The bowling game consists of n turns, and the number of pins in each
  turn is exactly 10.

  Assume a player hits xi pins in the i^th turn. The value of the i^th
  turn for the player is:

  - 2xi if the player hits 10 pins in either (i - 1)^th or (i - 2)^th
    turn.

  - Otherwise, it is xi.

  The score of the player is the sum of the values of their n turns.

  Return

  - 1 if the score of player 1 is more than the score of player 2,

  - 2 if the score of player 2 is more than the score of player 1, and

  - 0 in case of a draw.

CONSTRAINTS
  n == player1.length == player2.length
  1 <= n <= 1000
  0 <= player1[i], player2[i] <= 10

TEST CASES
  [1] Input    : player1 = [5,10,3,2], player2 = [6,5,7,3]
      Expected : 1
      Why      : The score of player 1 is 5 + 10 + 2*3 + 2*2 = 25.
                 The score of player 2 is 6 + 5 + 7 + 3 = 21.

  [2] Input    : player1 = [3,5,7,6], player2 = [8,10,10,2]
      Expected : 2
      Why      : The score of player 1 is 3 + 5 + 7 + 6 = 21.
                 The score of player 2 is 8 + 10 + 2*10 + 2*2 = 42.

  [3] Input    : player1 = [2,3], player2 = [4,1]
      Expected : 0
      Why      : The score of player1 is 2 + 3 = 5.
                 The score of player2 is 4 + 1 = 5.

  [4] Input    : player1 = [1,1,1,10,10,10,10], player2 = [10,10,10,10,1,1,1]
      Expected : 2
      Why      : The score of player1 is 1 + 1 + 1 + 10 + 2*10 + 2*10 + 2*10 = 73.
                 The score of player2 is 10 + 2*10 + 2*10 + 2*10 + 2*1 + 2*1 + 1 = 75.

CODE TYPE
  function
    driver: one Solution call per test case

SIGNATURE
  int isWinner(vector<int>& player1, vector<int>& player2);
