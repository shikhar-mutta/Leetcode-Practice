 3414. Maximum Score of Non-overlapping Intervals
 Hard  |  Tags: Array, Binary Search, Dynamic Programming, Sorting
 https://leetcode.com/problems/maximum-score-of-non-overlapping-intervals/description/
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PROBLEM
  You are given a 2D integer array intervals, where intervals[i] = [li,
  ri, weighti]. Interval i starts at position li and ends at ri, and has a
  weight of weighti. You can choose up to 4 non-overlapping intervals. The
  score of the chosen intervals is defined as the total sum of their
  weights.

  Return the lexicographically smallest array of at most 4 indices from
  intervals with maximum score, representing your choice of non-
  overlapping intervals.

  Two intervals are said to be non-overlapping if they do not share any
  points. In particular, intervals sharing a left or right boundary are
  considered overlapping.

CONSTRAINTS
  1 <= intevals.length <= 5 * 10^4
  intervals[i].length == 3
  intervals[i] = [li, ri, weighti]
  1 <= li <= ri <= 10^9
  1 <= weighti <= 10^9

TEST CASES
  [1] Input    : intervals = [[1,3,2],[4,5,2],[1,5,5],[6,9,3],[6,7,1],[8,9,1]]
      Expected : [2,3]
      Why      : You can choose the intervals with indices 2, and 3 with respective weights of 5, and 3.

  [2] Input    : intervals = [[5,8,1],[6,7,7],[4,7,3],[9,10,6],[7,8,2],[11,14,3],[3,5,5]]
      Expected : [1,3,5,6]
      Why      : You can choose the intervals with indices 1, 3, 5, and 6 with respective weights of 7, 6, 3, and 5.

CODE TYPE
  function
    driver: one Solution call per test case

SIGNATURE
  vector<int> maximumWeight(vector<vector<int>>& intervals);
