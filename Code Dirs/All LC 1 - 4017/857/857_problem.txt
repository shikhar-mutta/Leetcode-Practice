 857. Minimum Cost to Hire K Workers
 Hard  |  Tags: Array, Greedy, Sorting, Heap (Priority Queue)
 https://leetcode.com/problems/minimum-cost-to-hire-k-workers/description/
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PROBLEM
  There are n workers. You are given two integer arrays quality and wage
  where quality[i] is the quality of the i^th worker and wage[i] is the
  minimum wage expectation for the i^th worker.

  We want to hire exactly k workers to form a paid group. To hire a group
  of k workers, we must pay them according to the following rules:

  - Every worker in the paid group must be paid at least their minimum
    wage expectation.

  - In the group, each worker's pay must be directly proportional to their
    quality. This means if a worker’s quality is double that of another
    worker in the group, then they must be paid twice as much as the other
    worker.

  Given the integer k, return the least amount of money needed to form a
  paid group satisfying the above conditions. Answers within 10^-5 of the
  actual answer will be accepted.

CONSTRAINTS
  n == quality.length == wage.length
  1 <= k <= n <= 10^4
  1 <= quality[i], wage[i] <= 10^4

TEST CASES
  [1] Input    : quality = [10,20,5], wage = [70,50,30], k = 2
      Expected : 105.00000
      Why      : We pay 70 to 0^th worker and 35 to 2^nd worker.

  [2] Input    : quality = [3,1,10,10,1], wage = [4,8,2,2,7], k = 3
      Expected : 30.66667
      Why      : We pay 4 to 0^th worker, 13.33333 to 2^nd and 3^rd workers separately.

CODE TYPE
  function
    driver: one Solution call per test case

SIGNATURE
  double mincostToHireWorkers(vector<int>& quality, vector<int>& wage, int k);
